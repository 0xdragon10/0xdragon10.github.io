Offensive Security, Research & Engineering

Mohamed Haytham — known online as 0xdragon — works at the intersection of offensive security engineering and security research. The focus is not just finding a bug; it is understanding the underlying system, building an attack path, validating impact, and turning the result into something defensible.

The technical direction spans binary exploitation, reverse engineering, web and network security, Active Directory attack paths, reconnaissance, and red team operations.

Security research with the Synack Red Team is part of that trajectory, with an emphasis on continuous target research, attack-surface analysis, and finding the paths that are easy to miss when testing becomes purely tool-driven.
